Our club

More than ninety minutes.

Founded in 1996, CalBlue brings together players from across the Bay Area through serious competition and a lasting sense of belonging. The best teams are built through trust, consistency, and joy.

Mission

CalBlue strives to represent and promote the best of Chinese-American communities in competitive soccer in the U.S. and beyond.
